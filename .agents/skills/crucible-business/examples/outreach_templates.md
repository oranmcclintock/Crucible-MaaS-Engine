# Outreach Playbook: The Deterministic Legacy Migration Engine

- **Primary:** Delivery Managers, Delivery Leads at Mid-tier Systems Integrators (SIs) (50-500 employees)
- **Secondary:** Solution Architects, Lead Data Engineers

## Trigger Events
Monitor LinkedIn or Google Alerts for SIs that have recently won:
1. "Digital Transformation" or "AI-Readiness" contracts.

**Why this wins Delivery Managers:**
1. **Below the Radar:** £10k is usually under the Manager Approval limit (credit card expense, bypasses 6 weeks of procurement).
