# Target Personas & Signal Detection: The Rescue Mission Playbook

## The Core Strategy
You are not selling software. You are selling **Tactical Relief** to **Delivery Managers** who are actively bleeding margin on fixed-price legacy migrations. Procurement departments buy from mega-vendors; Delivery Managers buy immediate risk mitigation to save their weekends and their project margins.

## 1. The Target Decision-Makers
Focus exclusively on mid-sized IT Consultancies (50 - 500 employees). These SIs win lucrative public sector or healthcare contracts but often lack the elite, specialized internal data engineering talent required when things go wrong.

**Primary Targets:** 
- Delivery Manager
- Delivery Lead
- Project Director

**Secondary Targets (Technical Champions):**
- Solution Architect
- Lead Data Engineer

## 2. Reading Between the Lines: Signal Detection

You must identify the exact moment a project enters "Data Mapping Purgatory." Industry data shows that 83% of digital transformation projects stall or blow their budgets around **Month 6**, usually because the legacy database is infinitely more decayed than budgeted. 

### A. Press Releases (PR) & News
**The Signal:** An SI announces a major "Digital Transformation," "Cloud Migration," or "AI-Readiness" contract win.
**The Play:** 
- Do not pitch immediately. They are in the "honeymoon phase" and underestimate the dirty data tax.
- Log the announcement and set a calendar reminder for 5 to 6 months post-announcement.
- At Month 6, when they are drowning in undocumented schema decay, strike with the £10k Legacy Decay Audit ("Rescue Mission") pitch.

### B. Job Postings (The "Panic Hiring" Indicator)
**The Signal:** A mid-tier SI suddenly posts multiple openings for short-term/contract "Data Engineers," "ETL Developers," or "Legacy SQL Specialists."
**The Play:** 
- This is a definitive indicator of a stalled migration. They are attempting to throw expensive human capital at a programmatic problem. 
- Job descriptions highlighting "data cleansing," "handling messy data," or migrating from legacy systems mean they are actively bleeding margin.
- Reach out to the Delivery Manager immediately. Pitch the £10k Audit as a deterministic, fixed-price alternative to hiring £600/day contractors.

### C. LinkedIn Profiles & Activity
**The Signal:** 
- Delivery Managers or Architects posting about "lessons learned in data quality," the "hidden costs of technical debt," or the challenges of "AI readiness."
- Updates indicating a project has been in "Phase 1" or "Discovery" for an unusually long time.
**The Play:**
- Use the "AI-Readiness Hook" or the "Rescue Mission Hook" tailored to their public struggles. 
- Emphasize that your Deterministic Engine acts as a "Ghost Subcontractor," neutralizing the data decay without requiring access to their live PII.
